Queue a meeting utterance with complete content and a short spoken summary in speech_text. Optional mentions select the agents that should receive model input. The host fixes identity and scope; IDs are generated internally. Full text and summary stay in the speech queue until the playback worker grants ownership. The result has message.message_id=null, published=false, and speech_request.content containing the queued full text. Queue inspection does not send. After grant, the wrapper sends through existing Team/group messaging before audio starts; agents fetch the message normally. Cancellation or close before grant sends nothing. Interruption after sending retains the message. This call does not wait for playback.
